## Brand symbol

The **Shiro Hannya** mark is a vector redraw based on a reference by **monnik**
(Noun Project), as documented in the Oni brand book (page 10): new paths, empty
eye sockets, recentered and a kintsugi crack. The underlying shape remains based
on that work.

Before commercial use or trademark registration: **license the reference icon or
commission/produce a manual redraw**. The terminal glyph, wordmark and lockups
are original to this project.
